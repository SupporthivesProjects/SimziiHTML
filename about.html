OUR STORY

Why Simzii exists

Roaming bills used to be the souvenir nobody wanted. Simzii started with a simpler idea: buy the data before you fly, install it from your phone, and keep your own SIM for calls and texts.

We sell prepaid eSIMs for single countries and for whole regions. Every plan shows its data, its days and its price up front, so you know what you are paying for before you pay it.

When the trip changes, the account keeps up. Top up from your phone, add another destination, or download an invoice for the expense report.

Pick a plan, scan a code,
and get on with the trip.
